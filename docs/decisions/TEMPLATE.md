# ADR-NNNN: Title stating the decision, not the topic

- **Status:** accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context:** the forces at play — what problem, what constraints,
  what alternatives were live at the time. Write it so a stranger can
  disagree intelligently.
- **Decision:** one paragraph, active voice: "We will..."
- **Consequences:** what becomes easier, what becomes harder, what debt
  is consciously taken on. Honest consequences are the whole value.
